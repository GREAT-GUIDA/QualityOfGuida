This mod mainly features 7 Quality of Life items from Minecraft. 
None of them directly affect player combat.
Fully supports multiplayer gaming.
Made for the Mod Jam 2025 "Game Tribute".

[h2]Paper[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crafted using wood
[*]Right-click to edit its item description
[*]Used for sharing information or writing tutorials
[*]All items from this mod naturally spawn with a paper as an instruction manual
[*]Can also be used to craft paper airplanes
[/list]

[h2]Name Tag[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crafted using paper and copper/tin bars, also spawns in surface chests
[*]Used to rename creatures; renamed non-boss creatures won't naturally despawn
[*]Certain names have special effects
[*]Not consumed on use
[/list]

[h2]Flint and Steel[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crafted using hellstone and iron/lead bars, also spawns near abandoned portals on the surface
[*]Used to activate nether portals
[*]Supports smart cursor
[/list]

[h2]Bone Meal[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crafted using bones
[*]Use on blocks to make dirt grow plants and accelerate growth of various plants; works on most modded plants
[*]Supports smart cursor
[/list]

[h2]Torcherino[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crafted using ultra bright torch and fast clock
[*]Accelerates nearby block updates, also effective when held
[/list]

[h2]Spawn Egg[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crafted using light soul and dark soul
[*]Use empty spawn egg on enemies to convert it into corresponding enemy spawn egg
[*]Use again to consume spawn egg and spawn corresponding enemy
[*]Limited to enemies that have banners
[*]Additionally, surface and underground chests now spawn with some specific monster spawn eggs
[/list]

[h2]Spawner[/h2]
[list]
[*]Crafted using terrarium and chlorophyte bars, also spawns naturally underground
[*]After using spawn egg on spawner, it will continuously summon corresponding monsters
[*]Spawn rate is limited by natural spawn rate; rare enemies also have reduced spawn rates
[*]When mined, only drops some coins
[/list]


这个模组的内容主要是7个来自Minecraft的QoL物品，以及一些来自Minecraft的词缀，它们都不会直接影响玩家战斗
完全支持多人游戏
该mod是 Mod Jam 2025 "Game Tribute"参赛作品

[h2]纸[/h2]
[list]
[*]使用木头制作
[*]右键可以编辑其物品描述
[*]用于传递信息，或者写教程
[*]本mod的所有物品自然生成时都会附带一张纸作为说明书
[*]另外可以用来做纸飞机
[/list]

[h2]命名牌[/h2]
[list]
[*]使用纸和铜/锡锭制作，另外也会生成于地表的箱子中
[*]用于给生物重命名，被命名的非boss生物不会自然刷新
[*]部分名称有特殊效果
[*]不消耗
[/list]

[h2]打火石[/h2]
[list]
[*]使用狱石和铁/铅锭制作，也会自然生成于地表的废弃传送门边
[*]用于激活下界传送门
[*]支持智能光标
[/list]

[h2]骨粉[/h2]
[list]
[*]使用骨头制作
[*]对物块使用，让泥土长出植物，以及催熟各种植物，对大部分Mod植物也有效
[*]支持智能光标
[/list]

[h2]加速火把[/h2]
[list]
[*]使用超亮火把和快走时钟制作
[*]加速附近物块的更新，手持时也有效
[/list]

[h2]刷怪蛋[/h2]
[list]
[*]使用光明之魂和暗影之魂制作
[*]空刷怪蛋对敌人使用，可以转化为对应敌人刷怪蛋
[*]再次使用消耗刷怪蛋，生成对应敌人
[*]仅限拥有旗帜的敌人
[*]另外，地表和地下的箱子里现在会生成一些特定怪物的刷怪蛋
[/list]

[h2]刷怪笼[/h2]
[list]
[*]使用饲养笼和叶绿锭制作，也会在地下自然生成
[*]对刷怪笼使用刷怪蛋后，刷怪笼会持续召唤对应怪物
[*]召唤速率受自然生成率限制，稀有敌人召唤速率也会变低
[*]挖掘后只会掉落一些钱币
[/list]
